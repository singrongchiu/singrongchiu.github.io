Dinosaur Tapping Mini-Game
Scene & Objects:
- A charming, pixel-art farm environment with grassy fields, flowers, and small rocks. A friendly dinosaur stands in the center of the scene, animated with idle movements like blinking, tail swaying, or small hops. Background elements like trees and fences give the farm a cozy, Stardew Valley–style vibe.
Player Input:
- The player taps on the dinosaur to interact with it. Each tap triggers an animation, such as the dinosaur jumping, nodding, or making a sound.
Gameplay:
- The goal is to tap the dinosaur multiple times to reach a target number of interactions within the mini-game session. Each successful tap is visually acknowledged with sparkles, small icons, or a fun sound effect. The dinosaur may occasionally move around the screen slowly, adding a gentle challenge, but the overall pace is relaxed to fit the cozy farm aesthetic.
Win Condition:
 - The mini-game is completed when the player reaches the required 8 taps, at which point the dinosaur gives a happy animation or small reward, such as a “pet coin” or “heart” to reinforce the Stardew Valley–style charm.
